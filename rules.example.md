# My rules (example)

The four things to write down before you open Claude (step 1 of the guide). These are the ONLY reasons the scout may drop a role. Replace the bracketed values with your own, then paste them into the project instructions in `PROMPT.md`.

1. **Employment type.** I want [the kinds of work you want: full-time, part-time, contract, fractional]. Drop [the kinds you don't].
2. **Freshness.** Drop anything posted more than [7] days ago.
3. **Pay.** Drop it only if the confirmed rate is [your lowest hourly rate] an hour or less. If no pay is listed, keep it and flag it.
4. **Role.** It must be one of: [every role title you'd answer to]. Similar titles that clearly mean the same job count.

There is no fifth reason. Industry, company size, how senior it sounds, whether the job board says you're a match: none of those are reasons to drop a role. You decide those yourself.
