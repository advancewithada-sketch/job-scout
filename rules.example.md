# My rules (example)

The four things to write down before you open Claude (step 1 of the guide). These are the ONLY reasons the scout may drop a role. Replace the bracketed values with your own, then paste them into the project instructions in `PROMPT.md`.

1. **Employment type.** I want [part-time / contract / fractional]. Drop [full-time salaried].
2. **Freshness.** Drop anything posted more than [7] days ago.
3. **Pay.** Drop it only if the confirmed rate is [$50] an hour or less. If no pay is listed, keep it and flag it.
4. **Role.** It must be one of: [Chief Operating Officer, Director of Operations, Operations Manager, Online Business Manager, Head of Operations]. Similar titles that clearly mean the same job count.

There is no fifth reason. Industry, company size, how senior it sounds, whether the job board says you're a match: none of those are reasons to drop a role. You decide those yourself.

## Search phrases (for steps 3 and 4)

One plain search per phrase, because quoted phrases and location filters are often ignored:

- [fractional COO]
- [part time chief operating officer]
- [online business manager]
- [fractional director of operations]
- [part time operations manager remote]

Look in: [the US, Canada and remote worldwide]
